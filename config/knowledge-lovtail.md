## About
- Lovtail (lovtail.com) is a pet products brand by Balaji Home Decor Pvt. Ltd. - the same company as Auralivin, and it uses the same WhatsApp number.
- Sells: pet beds and pet sofas, pet tents, pet mats, pet feeders and bowls, pet accessories, and pet food.
- Contact: lovtailindia@gmail.com, call/WhatsApp 9520666401 (9am-7pm).
- Payment: online (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets via Razorpay) and Cash on Delivery where offered at checkout.

## Orders & shipping
- Free shipping on all orders across India - no delivery charges at checkout.
- Orders are processed within 1-3 business days after confirmation.
- Estimated delivery within India: 5-10 business days from dispatch. It can vary by location, product availability or courier delays.
- A tracking ID is sent by email/SMS once the order ships. Customers can also type "track" here and send their order number.
- Lovtail ships only within India.
- If a parcel comes back because of a wrong address or nobody being available after repeated attempts, re-delivery charges may apply.

## Returns, refunds, exchanges, cancellations
- 7-day easy returns: most products can be returned within 7 days of delivery, unused, in original condition with tags, packaging and accessories.
- Exchanges for a different size or colour are possible within the same 7 days, subject to stock.
- Damaged, defective or wrong item: tell us within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos or a video (including the outer box) - we arrange a replacement or full refund at no extra cost.
- Not returnable: custom/personalised products, clearance/final-sale items, opened pet food packs (hygiene), products used, washed, chewed or damaged after delivery.
- To return: email lovtailindia@gmail.com or WhatsApp 9520666401 within 7 days of delivery with the order number and reason.
- Refunds go to the original payment method within 7-10 business days after the returned product is inspected. COD refunds are paid by bank transfer.
- Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placing them if not yet dispatched. Custom orders can't be cancelled once production starts.
- Returns/refunds/exchanges are handled by our team - the bot should NOT promise or approve one, only explain the policy and let the team take over.
