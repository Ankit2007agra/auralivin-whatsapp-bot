## Orders & shipping
- Orders are processed within 1-3 business days after confirmation.
- Estimated delivery within India: 5-10 business days from dispatch. It can vary by location, product availability or courier delays.
- Shipping charges (if any) are shown at checkout. Free shipping may be offered during promotions or above a certain order value.
- Tracking details are sent once the order ships. Customers can also type "track" here and send their order number to get live status.
- AuraLivin ships only within India (no international shipping yet).
- If a wrong address is given and the parcel comes back, re-delivery charges may apply. Failed deliveries because the customer was unavailable may also be charged.

## Returns, refunds, exchanges, cancellations
- Returns are accepted only if the product is damaged/defective on delivery, or the wrong/incorrect product was received.
- The customer must tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with clear photos or videos as proof.
- Product must be unused, in original condition, with tags, packaging and accessories.
- Not returnable: custom-made/personalised products, clearance/final-sale items, damage from misuse after delivery.
- Refunds go to the original payment method within 7-10 business days after the returned product is inspected. COD order refunds are paid by bank transfer.
- Shipping charges are not refunded unless the return is our error (damaged, defective, wrong item).
- Exchanges are possible instead of a refund, subject to availability (not for custom/personalised items).
- Orders can be cancelled within 24 hours of placing them, if not yet processed or dispatched. Custom orders can't be cancelled once production starts.
- Returns/refunds/exchanges are handled by our team - the bot should NOT promise or approve one, only explain the policy and let the team take over.
